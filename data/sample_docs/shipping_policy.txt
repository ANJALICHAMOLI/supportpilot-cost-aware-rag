SHIPPING POLICY

1. Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email.

2. Shipping Rates and Delivery Estimates
- Standard Shipping (5-7 business days): $4.99, free on orders over $50
- Expedited Shipping (2-3 business days): $12.99
- Overnight Shipping (1 business day): $24.99

Delivery delays can occasionally occur due to customs, weather, or carrier issues.

3. Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking
You will receive a shipment confirmation email with a tracking number once your order has shipped. The tracking number will be active within 24 hours.

4. Customs, Duties, and Taxes
For international orders, the customer is responsible for any customs and duties fees that may be incurred. We are not responsible for delays caused by the customs department.

5. Damages
We are not liable for products damaged or lost during shipping. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier directly to file a claim.

6. International Shipping Policy
We currently ship to over 40 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on destination and are calculated at checkout.

7. Address Changes
If you need to change your shipping address, contact us within 1 hour of placing your order at support@example.com. Once an order has entered processing, the address cannot be changed.

8. Missing Packages
If your tracking information shows your package was delivered but you have not received it, please wait 24-48 hours as some packages may show as delivered before arrival. If it still hasn't arrived, contact our support team.
